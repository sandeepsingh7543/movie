  │ 
  │ 🎬 **Watchlist Management** — Add movies and web series with poster, genre, rating, mood, watch status, notes, and external links.

  │ 
  │ 📊 **Profile & Stats** — View your watching statistics including total movies, series, completed titles, episodes watched, total watch time, average rating, and completion rate.
  │

  │ 
  │ 🔗 **External Links** — Save streaming links for quick access and share them with friends.
  │ 
  │ 📝 **Notes** — Add personal notes and reviews for each title.
  │ 
  │ 🌙 **Dark Cinematic UI** — Stunning dark theme with gradient accents designed for movie lovers.

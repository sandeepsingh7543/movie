## App Description

Organize Your Personal Arab Movie Collection

Arab Film House is your personal movie organizer designed specifically for Arab cinema enthusiasts. Create, manage, and organize your own curated collection of Arab films with this powerful cataloging tool.

Key Features:

Personal Movie Database
• Add movies manually with custom details
• Upload your own movie posters and images
• Rate and review films in your collection
• Organize by genre, year, and director

Cast & Crew Management
• Add cast members with photos and roles
• Track directors, actors, and production crew
• Build comprehensive film profiles

Smart Organization
• Search through your entire collection
• Filter by genre, rating, or year
• Create personal watchlists
• Browse by categories

Offline Access
• All data stored locally on your device
• No internet required after setup
• Complete privacy - your data stays with you
• No account registration needed

Perfect For:
• Arab cinema enthusiasts
• Film students and researchers
• Movie collectors and archivists
• Anyone building a personal film library

Important Note:
This app is a personal cataloging tool only. Users manually add all movie information and images. The app does not stream, host, or provide access to copyrighted content. All content responsibility lies with the user.

Start building your personal Arab cinema collection today!
